Christian Calle

Learning designer, game maker and founder of CourseKiln.

For twenty years I've been turning hard material into learning people can use: first as a teacher and program director in South Korea, then designing technical training for security-screening equipment used around the world. Along the way I taught myself to code, so I could build the games and simulations my learners needed.

What I'm working on

CourseKiln

I'm building CourseKiln, browser-based tools for making interactive courses and keeping them up to date in any learning management system. One fix reaches every copy of a course, wherever it's been published. The free beta opens on January 11, 2027.

Visit coursekilnapp.com

Rapiscan Systems

I lead learning experience and training strategy for teams around the world: AI-assisted course production, keeping the curriculum consistent across countries, and tying training to how the equipment and its operators actually perform.
