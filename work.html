X-ray equipment and systems simulations

Made with Articulate Storyline, custom JavaScript, Adobe Creative Cloud and Unity

Simulations of x-ray screening equipment and its software, so people learn the controls safely before working on live systems. Custom JavaScript tracks each learner's accuracy, speed and every step as they go.

The result: shorter onboarding on the hardware, and fewer operator errors once people are on the job. (The modules belong to my employers, so they're described here rather than shown.)

AI-assisted course production

Made with Microsoft Copilot, Adobe Firefly and Articulate 360

A faster way to produce courses: AI drafts the structure of a course for the designer to shape, and generates pictures that fit the subject, then the course is built in the usual authoring tools.

The result: complex courses produced in less time and looking better, so teams get training updates as quickly as their operations change.

Equipment walkthrough videos

Made with Adobe Premiere Pro and After Effects

Animated videos explaining equipment components, maintenance steps and how data moves through a system, with clear callouts and pacing for technical audiences where precision matters.

Field teams use them on demand, which improved accuracy after training and cut equipment downtime.

Teaching and curriculum in Korea

From 2006 to 2017 I taught English in South Korea, to everyone from kindergarteners to adults. Along the way I:

  • Joined the opening teams of three English immersion centers, where students learn in rooms built like a restaurant, a supermarket or a hospital, and wrote almost all of the program content for one of them.
  • Worked as a teacher, then head teacher, then Education Director at Incheon English Village.
  • Created the content for public-school summer and winter English camps, and put together the camp workbooks, including their artwork.
  • Coordinated a professional development program for the district's teachers: preparing the training materials and moderating the sessions.
  • Made games for my own classes, which you can still play here.
